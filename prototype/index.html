EzTimeZone

prototype

Drag any strip sideways — all five move together. The amber line is the moment you’ve picked. Pinch or scroll to zoom.

What this prototype covers

  • One shared instant drives all rows — hour ticks are computed per zone, so half-hour offsets (Kolkata, Kathmandu) sit where they really are.
  • DST transitions inside the visible window are found by binary search, so tick grids stay correct across a spring-forward.
  • Amber band = 09:00–17:00 local. Dark band = 22:00–07:00 local.
  • Zones and the picked time persist in this browser and encode into the URL via Share.
  • Settling snaps to the nearest 15 minutes. Clock format toggles between 12h and 24h; morning hours are teal, afternoon and evening violet, on both the chips and the strip labels.
